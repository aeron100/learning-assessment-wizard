About & privacy

Frameworks & privacy

Bloom’s taxonomy

This tool uses Anderson and Krathwohl’s established 2001 revision: Remember, Understand, Apply, Analyze, Evaluate, and Create, alongside factual, conceptual, procedural, and metacognitive knowledge. New AI-focused adaptations are not a universally adopted replacement.

Read UC Davis’s taxonomy guide ↗

VARK modalities

Visual, Aural, Read/write, and Kinesthetic describe activity modalities here, not fixed student types. Selecting modalities does not diagnose a learner or imply that matching instruction to a preference improves achievement. Offer accessible alternatives that preserve the learning construct.

Read VARK’s framework guidance ↗

How drafts work

Drafts are generated by a free local Qwen2.5 model through WebLLM. Confirm subject accuracy, appropriate scope, objective coverage, and performance criteria before use. Suggested mastery thresholds are editable starting points.

Data and files

Course content is processed in this tab. There is no analytics, account, database, saved course text, or external generation service. Public model files are cached by WebLLM; use “Remove cached model” to delete them. Downloads save copies on your device. Your hosting provider and model-download providers may log requests and IP addresses; course text is not included in those requests.

Imports are limited by type, size, and parsing time. Only document text is extracted. Active content and suspicious control characters are rejected. No file checker guarantees a file is safe. Unusual academic language, multilingual text, and discussion of sensitive topics are not treated as misconduct.

AI-resilient assessment

Process checkpoints, course-specific evidence, and individual explanation make learning more observable. They do not make an assignment AI-proof. This tool does not use AI detectors or infer misconduct.
